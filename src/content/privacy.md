This is what Triquet keeps about the people who use it, where it keeps it, who can see it, and how to have it deleted. "We" are the people who run Triquet.

## What we keep

* **Your handle**, and the name you give beside it.
* **What you write**: your hunts and quizzes, the questions and answers in them, your reviews, and the answers a bot gave when asked. Also which hunts you are on, and your role on each.
* **If you sign in with Google**: the email address, name and picture Google sends us, and Google's identifier for your account. We ask Google for nothing more, so Triquet cannot read your mail, your files or anything else in your Google account.
* **Your sessions**: a record of each browser you use Triquet in, so that it stays signed in.

All of it is kept in Triquet's database, which [Convex](https://www.convex.dev) hosts. [Vercel](https://vercel.com) serves the pages. Like any web service, they keep logs of requests and errors, which we read to find and fix faults.

## Who sees what

* **A hunt's members**, its smiths and reviewers, see one another's handles and names, and the hunt's quizzes, as their role allows.
* **A hunt's address** begins with the handle of whoever made it (`/~handle/…`), so anyone given a link to it reads that handle. Someone who opens it without being on the hunt sees the names and handles of its smiths, so they know whom to ask to join.
* **Your review** of a quiz is yours alone until you share it. Then the hunt's smiths see it, and so does each of its reviewers who has shared their own.
* **Your email address** is shown to you alone.
* **We** can read the database, to keep Triquet running and to answer what you write to us.

Nobody else. We sell nothing, show no ads, and run no analytics or tracking.

## What stays in your browser

* **Your session** is kept in your browser's storage. Triquet sets no cookies, except that signing in with Google sets a few short-lived ones, to carry the sign-in to Google and back.
* **A history of the hunts you work on**, what you could see of them as they changed, is kept there too, for you to download as a hunt's Full History.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes both. If you have not signed in with Google, your handle belongs to that session alone: clearing it, or losing the browser, leaves the handle out of your reach for good.

## Asking a bot

When someone working on a quiz asks a bot about a question, a prompt made from the question and its quiz goes from our server to [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com), whose AI model answers it. The answer is kept with the question. Anthropic handles what it is sent under its own [privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Nothing is sent to a bot unless someone asks.

## Deleting your data

Write to [info@triquet.me](mailto:info@triquet.me), naming your handle, and say what you want deleted. If you sign in with Google, write from that address. We will delete it and write back to say so. A quiz that others on a hunt are working on belongs to the hunt as well, so we will ask before deleting one. This is the way to do it until Triquet can do it for you.
