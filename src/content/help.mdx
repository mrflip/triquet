## Standalone and verified accounts

You can start with a standalone account: a name and a handle, and nothing more. A verified account has a handle too, and you sign in to it through another service that vouches for you: Google, for now. A standalone account has these limits:

* Only the browser you made it in holds it. There is no password, so it can't be opened on another device, and it's lost if that browser's data for Triquet is cleared.
* It makes one hunt, labelled with its handle, which it can't relabel or delete.
* It can't bring anyone else onto its hunt, or be put on anyone else's.
* It can't review quizzes, its own included.

To verify your account, choose *Continue with Google* from the menu under your initial, at the top right. You keep your handle and your hunt, and the limits are lifted.
