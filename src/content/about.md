Triquet is a workbench for writing trivia quizzes, including the ones with a meta puzzle: a second layer that comes into view as the first answers come in. It keeps a quiz's questions and answers, lets a team of writers edit and refine them together, and checks each question for fairness and difficulty: playtesters review it, and an AI model can be asked to answer it as a player might.

Anyone can start with only a handle. If you verify your account with Google, Triquet asks Google for your name, email address and picture, and nothing more, so that your handle follows you to any device. What Triquet keeps, and who sees it, is in the [privacy policy](/privacy); the [terms of use](/terms) are short. Write to [info@triquet.me](mailto:info@triquet.me) with any question.
