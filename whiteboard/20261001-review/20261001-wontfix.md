## Won't fix (author's call)

- **Q3, the 438-yard li.** The Eastern Zhou li is usually given as about 416 m (455 yards). The figure is reverse-engineered on purpose so the answer comes out to exactly two years; "hardly" covers it.
- **Q3 hint, the Enterprise details.** All valid, none likely to trip a solver:
  - "inverse unit" is loose (the hertz is the unit of inverse seconds)
  - the 9,500 locations belong to Enterprise Mobility, the parent of several brands
  - "about three dozen each" is unverified
  - CHI is a city code, not an airport code like ATL and DEN; Dallas-Fort Worth is the only one of the four without capitals
- **Q6, "rampant gules".** León's lion is formally purpure, but the pictured seal shows it red, and the clue describes the picture.
- **Q8, "one Doomsday" (and "perpetual").** Deliberate. They are there for the solver who asks why they are there: 1998 and 2026 share a calendar, so a tenacious puzzler can work back to the weekday of April 10.
- **Q8 hint, present tense for Dolly Parton.** Left as is. The public mood is celebration rather than mourning.
- **Q9, "two associated locks".** Sources disagree (Wikipedia counts a pair above the wheel; a canal gazetteer lists a third below it). Sticking with two.
- **Q9 hint, "progenitors".** Ewes, Finn Dorset, mammary cells and the like are handled as also-accepts.
- **Q6, any of three answers vs. the chain.** Covered by the ground rules. The revised Q7 hint now names all three.
