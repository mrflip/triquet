# 2026 1DS "this BUT NOT that": early playtest feedback, by playtester

@TirrellA, @CrewB (Rundle B), @AnkenmanJ, and @VolkJ2 overall; Rundle B champs CrewB (1), LevineE2 (5th), MeehanR (15th)
Rundle C champs LeighJ2 (8th!) and BrownE12 (60th); Rundle D champ MotleyM2, and top-100 R-Rundlers MartinCH, LiputS, WilliamsM42.

https://learnedleague.com/profiles.php?76424

The playtesters not only got OP difficulty questions, I footgunned the process. Playtesters **are not shown the Smith's npte** -- you have to remember on your own to paste it in to Q1. Once the first couple came in I PM'ed everyone to look, but that must have been incredibly frustrating
I've included here one specific improvements they effected, but of course there were many behind it:e
* SzafranskiTJ -- motivated huge improvements to Francis Scott Key Fitzgerald, Parker/Clyde, and several oopsies.
* DunhamN and CarvellD both clearly invested significant time into detailed fact and copywriting checks -- not the "check on the details" kind but the "I'm going to establish the ground truth of each piece of this" kind. ("Dolly Parton [did not operate, but] co-owned Dollywood, but Herschend Family Entertainment operates it. Consider "theme park namesake" or "co-owner.", "The Wheel joins the Union Canal to the Forth and Clyde Canal, not to the Firth of Forthd" and several more of that caliber)
* MateoC gave valuable feedback on the difficulty levels and several good guesses for "could this be the thread rule" that I made sure to disarm
* RupertN gave difficulty calibration on each question and highlighted several places with confusing wording.
* NielsenE gave it a try and alerted me to the problem
* RyanBN and BuntingI both helped me understand I needed to identify a path through the solve: easy clues feeding harder clues so that the chain could assemble


The feedback was given on an early 15-question version. Each comment is condensed and paired with what changed in the version uploaded to the league site.

Changes that answer several testers at once:

- **The loop.** Fifteen questions became twelve, in a single closed loop.
- **The mechanism.** The Smith's note now has a five-question warm-up with its answers and a diagram, and Q1 opens with "READ THE SMITH'S NOTE".
- **The hidden ordering.** It is now defined (add up the numbers in each clueing), but nothing new in the questions signals it. The note is still to be rewritten.
